Privacy Policy for ReadyZoom

Effective date: June 18, 2026

ReadyZoom is a simple camera magnifier app for quickly viewing small labels, dim text, and fine details.

Information the app stores locally

The app can save zoom and light preferences on your device. If you choose to capture an image, the image and its editable title are stored inside the app on your device.

The app does not require an account and does not send your captures, titles, zoom settings, or light settings to a developer-operated server.

Camera and captures

The app requests camera permission to show the live magnifier view and create optional captures. Captures are stored inside the app by default.

The app does not request microphone, contacts, location, photo library, or broad storage access.

Advertising

The app may show a bottom banner ad in the main app. The app uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google Mobile Ads SDKs may collect device, advertising, diagnostics, and ad interaction data as described by Google.

ReadyZoom does not send your saved captures, capture titles, or camera preview content to Google for ad targeting.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its advertising services at:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Accounts

The app does not allow users to create accounts and does not support logging in with an external account.

Data deletion

Because the app does not create user accounts or store data on a developer-operated server, there is no account deletion process. You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app from your device.

If you have a privacy or deletion request, contact us at the email address below. For data handled by Google advertising services, use the privacy controls provided by Google and Android, including Android advertising ID controls.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior or third-party services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
