Privacy Policy for Glass Skin Routine

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Glass Skin Routine is a K-beauty focus, ingredient, layering, local Shelf, and read-only Daily routine helper app for Android and iOS.

Information stored on your device

Glass Skin Routine stores app data locally on your device. Skincare data remains local. The app has no account, login, analytics SDK or developer-operated tracking SDK. Optional feedback uses a separate developer-operated server.

Local data may include your selected skin goal or focus state, product names you add to Shelf, product category, optional product note, morning/evening usage flags, product timestamps, ingredient family IDs reserved for future local use, and a local legacy migration marker.

The current local storage keys include glass_skin_saved_tray_v1, glass_skin_shelf_products_v1, glass_skin_shelf_recovery_v1, glass_skin_saved_guides_v1, and glass_skin_legacy_migration_v1.

If Shelf data is damaged, readable products remain available. Before replacing damaged data, the app preserves the original locally for recovery. These copies can contain earlier product names and notes even after editing or deleting a visible product. They are not sent to our server or logged. Clearing the app’s storage in Android settings removes these copies together with other local app data.

Legacy product migration

If you update from an older Glass Skin Routine app, the current app may read old local app data on the same device only to import saved products into the current Shelf.

The migration is local-only. It is intended to import product-like data such as product names, categories, notes, and morning/evening use flags. It does not intentionally import check-ins, completion history, streaks, reminders, progress state, or Glow state.

Ads and consent

The app is a free app supported by Google Mobile Ads banner ads. It includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow.

Google Mobile Ads and UMP may process device, advertising, consent, diagnostic, and ad delivery information according to Google's advertising and privacy policies. Glass Skin Routine does not intentionally send your Shelf product names, product notes, selected focus, or Daily routine helper contents to a developer-operated server, and it does not intentionally pass those Shelf/focus details to the ad SDK.

Where required, the app may show a consent message before requesting ads. If consent or ad initialization fails, the app should continue to work and may simply show no banner.

Data transmission

In the app release, Glass Skin Routine does not intentionally transmit your focus choices, Shelf products, notes, or Daily routine helper data off your device.

The app does not include analytics SDK, developer-operated tracking SDK, backend sync, or account login.

Optional feedback is the limited exception described below. Other network features require updated disclosures before release.

Permissions

The app release does not request access to your camera, photos, face data, contacts, location, microphone, SMS, Health Connect, health records, or tracking permission.

Privacy and safety boundaries

Glass Skin Routine provides general cosmetic guidance, skin goals chosen by the user, ingredient clues, and layering cues.

The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, skin scoring, face scan, product selling, product recommendations, affiliate links, or guaranteed beauty outcomes.

For serious, persistent, or medical skin concerns, consult a qualified professional.

Optional Feedback

Only when you tap Send, the app sends your selected feedback category and message, app version/build, Android or iOS platform, display language, and a randomly generated submission receipt to MeOnMesh over HTTPS. The receipt identifies a submission, not a person or device. The current form does not attach a guide ID; a future contextual form must disclose that separately.

Feedback is used to review issues and improve the app. It is stored in a private administrator inbox on the MeOnMesh server, not forwarded by email or published. No name, email, account or stable device identifier is requested. Shelf products, product notes, routines, focus choices and search history are not attached. Please do not include personal, health or other sensitive information in free text. We cannot provide individual replies without contact information.

Feedback has a 90-day retention period and can be removed sooner by the administrator. Expired records are removed by scheduled cleanup and before inbox reads. Server snapshots, if configured by the hosting provider, may have separate retention. The feedback database does not store IP addresses; the web server temporarily uses IP addresses for abuse rate limiting. Feedback-route access logs are disabled, but infrastructure security logs may exist.

An unsent draft stays in application memory while the app is running; it is not saved to local storage. Closing the app may discard it. No draft is transmitted before Send. Network failures keep the draft for retry while the app remains running.

Request deletion of Glass Skin Routine feedback

You can delete products from Shelf inside the app. You can also clear local app data through your device settings. If you delete or uninstall the app, local app data may be removed by the operating system depending on your device settings.

There is no account to delete. Feedback follows the 90-day retention above. You can contact us about deletion; locating an entry without an account or email may require its approximate date and details.

  1. Email meonmesh@gmail.com with the subject “Glass Skin Routine feedback deletion”.
  2. Include the approximate submission date, feedback category, and enough of your original message to locate it. Do not send extra sensitive information.
  3. When we can identify the entry, the administrator deletes the original feedback message and its associated submitted metadata. Because submissions have no account or email attached, we may need clarification to identify the correct entry.

A minimal receipt/digest can remain until the original 90-day expiry to prevent a repeated submission from recreating deleted text. Provider snapshots, if any, may have separate retention. We cannot delete data held independently by Google advertising services; use Google and Android privacy controls for that data. Clearing app storage removes your local Shelf data and recovery copies, separately from server feedback.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
