A familiar grid, an extra challenge

Match the sum in each outlined cage while filling each row, column, and 3×3 box with 1–9.

Learn at your own pace

Open the rules guide or step-by-step tutorial, work through numbered levels, or try Easy and Medium Free play.

Room to think

Follow selected-cage shading and sum labels. Use pencil notes, Erase, Undo, and Redo. Progress stays on this device.

Good to know

No account is required. Progress is saved on this device, without cloud sync. The app includes banner advertising; there are no full-screen ads during puzzles.
