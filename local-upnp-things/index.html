Smart Home Status

Smart Home Status checks supported accessories already in Apple Home against your chosen On/Off goals. Review selected changes and read fresh responses after applying them.

This is the owner-selected release name for the iOS app previously called Local UPnP Things. Existing support and privacy URLs remain valid.

Apple Home checks and optional local tools

  • Create on-device power checklists for supported lights, plugs and switches.
  • Check current responses, select changes, review and Apply, then confirm new responses.
  • Use compatible HAP/HomeKit and Matter accessories already in Apple Home through Apple's HomeKit API. No independent Matter pairing is included.
  • Home reads are available on iOS 17; reviewed Home power changes require iOS 18 or later.
  • Home permission is optional. Local UPnP/SOAP tools remain available for compatible devices on the same Wi-Fi/LAN, including selected legacy Wemo models.
  • No developer-operated cloud account is required. No Home data is supplied as advertising context. Local tools may show consent-gated AdMob ads.

Checks describe responses at the time of a check, not continuous monitoring, physical power measurement or a home-safety guarantee. Compatibility depends on exposed services and characteristics.
