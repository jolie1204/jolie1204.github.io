Made for the way you use it.

Make room for important dates

Keep birthdays, trips, anniversaries, and deadlines together. Add an optional time, repeat events yearly, and search your dates.

Take a glance at your home screen

Choose one event for a Small widget or your next three upcoming events in Medium. Preview your choice in the app’s widget setup.

Choose your reminder

Set an optional reminder on the day or 1, 7, or 30 days before. Event data stays on your device, with manual JSON backup and restore.

Good to know

Reminder delivery depends on your device settings. Date-only reminders use 9 AM local time. Open the app at least once a year to renew yearly reminders. Widget titles may be visible on your home screen; notes are not shared with widgets. The app contains advertising.
