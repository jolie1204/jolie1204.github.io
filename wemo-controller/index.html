What happened to the Wemo app?

On January 31, 2026, Belkin discontinued the Wemo app and its cloud services. App control, remote access, and voice assistant integrations stopped working. But the devices themselves still respond to commands on your local network — they never needed the cloud for basic on/off. Wemo Controller talks to them directly over your LAN.

Features

  • Discover compatible Wemo devices on your home Wi-Fi, or add a device manually by local IP address.
  • Turn supported plugs and switches on or off; view and adjust dimmer levels.
  • Create schedules, auto-off timers, ON/OFF time windows, Away Mode, and sunrise/sunset rules.
  • Organize devices with favorites, rooms, and custom order.
  • Android home-screen widgets and Quick Settings tiles for saved local devices.
  • Review timing, affected devices, and possible conflicts before saving a rule.
  • Keep private on-device rules backups with guarded synchronization and recovery flows.
  • No cloud account. Your device data stays on your phone.

FAQ

Is there a Wemo app alternative after the shutdown?

Yes. Wemo Controller is a free Android alternative that controls Wemo devices locally, without Belkin's cloud.

Do my Wemo devices still work after January 31, 2026?

If they are still connected to your home Wi-Fi, yes — they respond to local network commands even though the official app is gone. Do not factory-reset them: Wi-Fi setup requires the discontinued app.

Can I control Wemo devices without the Wemo app?

Yes. Your phone just needs to be on the same Wi-Fi or LAN as the devices. The app does not provide control from outside your home network.

Does it work with Alexa or Google Home voice control?

No. Voice integrations depended on Belkin's cloud services and stopped working with the shutdown.

Which devices are supported?

Compatible legacy Wemo smart plugs, light switches, dimmers, and Insight models. Support depends on the device model, firmware, and available local services.

Privacy

Local device names, IP addresses, rooms, and troubleshooting details stay on your phone unless you choose to share diagnostics. The app does not send your Wemo device data to a developer-operated server. The app contains Google-served ads; basic local control remains free and ad loading does not block your controls.
